# Human Copywriting VI

Write like a real Vietnamese product/community operator.

## Goals

Copy should be:

- natural
- concise
- context-aware
- easy to scan
- specific
- appropriate to the relationship between bot, staff and member

## Avoid AI-smelling copy

Avoid unless genuinely needed:

- "Chào mừng bạn đến với..."
- "Chúng tôi rất vui mừng..."
- "Hãy cùng khám phá..."
- "Nâng tầm trải nghiệm..."
- excessive motivational language
- too many adjectives
- repeated sentence patterns
- overly polished corporate wording
- explaining obvious UI actions
- fake enthusiasm
- overuse of em dashes and decorative punctuation

## Before writing

Determine:

1. Who is speaking?
2. Who is reading?
3. What should the reader do next?
4. How much context is actually needed?
5. What tone fits this surface?

## Discord copy

Prefer short, direct copy.

Buttons:
- use action verbs
- keep labels short
- avoid full sentences

Embeds:
- one clear title
- short body
- structured fields only when useful
- avoid walls of text

Errors:
- explain what failed
- explain what the user can do next
- do not expose stack traces or internals

## Konoha voice

The Konoha project may use Japanese-inspired decoration, but readability comes first.

Do not force anime references into every sentence.

Keep the tone stylish, friendly and composed rather than childish or theatrical unless explicitly requested.

## Final pass

Before finishing user-facing copy, remove:

- redundant phrases
- filler words
- repeated meaning
- unnatural formality
- sentences that sound translated from English
